Common questions

How do I get an account?
Accounts are created by your company. Site administrators are set up by the account owner, and competent persons are set up by an administrator. If you need access, ask your site administrator rather than registering yourself.
How do I delete my account?
If you registered the account yourself, open Settings › Danger Zone › Request account deletion. If your account was created for you by an administrator, ask them to remove it.

Logbooks, daily logs, signatures and check-ins you filed are construction compliance documents. New York City Department of Buildings record-keeping requires the site to keep them, and they remain with the project. They keep your name on them, because a filed attestation has to say who made it.
Do workers need the app?
No. Workers check in by tapping the NFC tag at the site gate with their own phone, which opens a web page. No app, no account and no download is required.
My logbook says it has not synced.
The app saves your work to the phone first, so nothing is lost when the signal drops. Open the app somewhere with a connection and it will finish sending on its own. If a log still shows as unsynced after that, email us before uninstalling or signing out.
What permissions does the app use?
Camera for jobsite and certification photos attached to logs. Photo library for attaching existing photos. NFC for programming the check-in tags at a site gate, which is an administrator task. The app does not use your location or your microphone.

Privacy

LeveLog collects the information needed to produce compliance records: names, contact details, jobsite photos and signatures. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell personal information.
